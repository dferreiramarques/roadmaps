What you get

A roadmap people can actually read.

Three columns, clear tags, nothing to configure. Built so customers see where you're heading — and you stay in control of what they see.

Now · Next · Later

The simplest honest roadmap: what you're working on, what's coming, and what's further out — without promising dates.

Private by default

Every roadmap starts private. Draft it, reorder it, rewrite it — nobody sees it until you decide.

One click to public

Make it public and get a page to link to plus a snippet for your website. Switch it back to private whenever you like.

Your Drive, your data

Fast to edit

Matches your site

How it works

From idea to a public roadmap in four steps.

Sign in with Google

One click, no new password. Roadmaps gets a private folder in your Drive.

Add your features

Create a roadmap for your product and fill the three columns.

Make it public

Press Share → Make public. You get a page address and an embed snippet.

Paste and forget

Add the snippet to your site. Every edit you make here shows up there.

Show it anywhere

Two lines on your website.

Every public roadmap has an address on Roadmaps and a snippet that points back to it, so the link is always the same and always up to date.

<div data-roadmap="YOUR-ROADMAP-ID"
     data-header="true"></div>
<script src="https://<this-site>/embed.js"
        defer></script>
https://<this-site>/r/YOUR-ROADMAP-ID

# a page anyone can open, to link from
# your site, emails or release notes
No middleman

Roadmaps doesn't keep your data. Google does.

There's no Roadmaps database. Your roadmaps are files in a private folder of your Google Drive, and the public copy of a roadmap exists only while you keep it shared.

No server of ours

Your roadmaps go straight from your browser to your Drive. We only help you sign in and show public roadmaps to your visitors.

Yours to revoke

Make a roadmap private and its public page and embeds stop working immediately. Remove access in your Google Account and everything goes with it.
